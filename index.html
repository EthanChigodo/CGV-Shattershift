    HOW THEY PLAY

    ♕King of the Hill

    Stand on the helipad alone and its clock is yours; two or more on it and nobody scores. Knock rivals off it, off the open ledges, into the hatches. First to the target time wins.

    ⚑Capture the Flag

    Blue (P1, P3) against red (P2, P4) - one on one with two. Run onto the other team's core to take it, and bring it to your plinth while yours is still home. Carrying it you're slower and can't throw; any hit and you drop it. A teammate touching your dropped core sends it home. First to 3 captures.
