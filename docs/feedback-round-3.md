# Feedback round 3 - the team's playtest

What the team raised after round 2, and what changed.

| # | Feedback | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Roof zombies just fall down when they die, and stay | A downed enemy **burns away to ash**: the hit throws them back, arms flung wide, and from the head down they dissolve through noise with ember-bright edges, sparks and ash coming off - gone in 1.5 s (`roof.js` `ashify()` / `updateDown()`, the `enemy-ash` event). The dissolve is a patch on the enemy's own copies of its materials (still a heat signature through the Thermal Aegis); it is compiled during the roof's load, so the first one down doesn't stall the fight. |
| 2 | A game save: continue or start a new game; the difficulty on the next screen | The main menu's first button is **Continue** when there is a save (it says where and at what difficulty), then **New game**, which opens its own screen with the three difficulties as cards (and a note that it replaces the save). The story saves each time it reaches a sector - with the score, spheres and difficulty - and clears it when you get out (`src/systems/story-save.js`). Chapters don't touch the save; the difficulty picker moved from the loadout card to the Chapters screen. |
| 3 | The lifts are sometimes metal, sometimes glass | All glass: the Labs' arrival lift and the roof's lift housing (`meltdown/elevator.js`) now have the Calibration Lift's look - glass doors with a lit edge, glass walls in steel mullions, conduit posts, a dark shaft behind - and the ride after the Labs is in the Gravity Fault's glass cabin. |
| + | The lifts should be attached to something, not run into nowhere | The glass cabin hangs from **hoist ropes** over a crosshead and sheave (they whip away up the shaft when the cable snaps in the Gravity Fault); the Calibration Lifts have a crosshead, a sheave and ropes running up the glass tube to a **machine head** at the top, its drum turning as they climb. |
| 4 | The skin tone looks coloured over; no eyes in the lift scene after the Labs - make it first person | The ride after the Labs is now **first person**: the doors close on the burning Labs (and Okoro), you turn to the glass and the city burning below, slide down the door wall, his **radio in your hand** for the pilot's two lines, take the launcher from where it leans on the glass, the tower shudders, and the doors open on the skybridge's light (`quiet-ride.js`). The skin re-tint (`figure/look.js`) now picks up skin in shadow and highlight too (it judged warmth by raw difference, so dark and bright skin kept the model's own tone - the patchy look) and scales each pixel channel by channel, so lips, cheeks and irises keep their own variation. |
| 5 | Health bar to the top left, and bigger | The vitals panel (integrity, air, the ECG) is at the **top left** under the title in every level, and bigger; the Labs and roof's integrity and spheres are there too. The bottom readouts (spheres, score, combo) are bigger; the Foundry's route panel moved under the vitals. |
| 6 | Cryo should act like shock with the ice animation; no frozen floor unless it hit the floor | Cryo now **goes off like shock**, in ice: it shatters what's breakable within 4.8 m (shock still one-hits reinforced glass), still puts out fires and freezes debris and machinery. The frost patch and ice spikes appear **only when it burst on the floor**; on a pane, a wall or in the air it is the ice burst alone. |
| 7 | A loading screen instead of black | **Ascension Tower, drawing itself**: its windows light floor by floor from the street up as the models stream in (the floor counter climbs with it), the bottom floors burning, a scan line sweeping the facade, glass shards drifting past, the city's silhouette at its feet, the sector you're headed for in big glitching letters, a progress bar and rotating tips (`src/ui/loading-screen.js`). It comes up when the screen has been black a moment and something is loading or being built - never over a cutscene's own fade to black. Progress is three.js's loading manager. |
| 8 | A cooler sign that the fire got you (and a death animation per level) | Each death **plays out before the end screen**, made from the last frame (`src/ui/death-fx.js`): **Labs** - the fire actually sweeps over you (the fire front runs past the runner for a second and a half) and the screen burns, flames climbing it in the heat haze; **Skyline** - cracks race out and the picture shatters like a pane, the shards falling away (fire burns, a fall drops away); **Foundry** - the hit shakes the screen and two hazard-striped press slabs slam shut, sparks from the seam; **roof** - three claws rake across, a red pulse, passing out; going off a ledge, the picture drops away into the dark. |
| 9 | Balls travel too far after a hit; thrown by hand should arc shorter than the gun | Every hit takes speed off (walls 50 %, solids 30 %, panes they punch through 60 %, the floor's bounce and friction harder), they slow in the air once they've hit something and are spent within a second (`SPHERE_FLIGHT`, shared by every level). Thrown by hand (the Foundry) they are 28 % slower with a real arc; the launcher still fires them flat. |
| 10 | The ending is different per difficulty (Hard: the helicopter lands on the pad; Normal: you jump) | **One ending**: the helicopter can never land on the roof coming apart - it always hangs off the east ledge and you jump for the ladder. (Clearing the roof still brings it in early, and still scores the bonus.) |
| 11 | Integrate the sound and voices from the fork; shorter subtitles | Merged `siphokazi-tech6/CGV-Shattershift` (every voiced line, the patients, the music per stage, the sound effects). A recorded line now takes exactly as long as it is said - the next follows straight on, no reading-time gap after it - and its subtitle goes as the words end; the Skyline's intercom panel likewise. |
| 12 | Entering the lift with the shield leaves the shield behind | The shield bubble rides up with you in the lift. |
| 13 | Remove the "containment" title that pops up at the top | The Skyline's beat titles ("CONTAINMENT WARD" and the rest) are gone. |
| 14 | The air ducts are open on top - you walk on air | The fallen ducts have a steel **lid** and end caps. |
| 15 | A cooler briefing, and better performance | The briefing is now **Okoro's recording**: a camera overlay (REC light, a running timecode from 23:41 tonight, the evidence number, corner marks), film grain and a vignette; each chapter opens on its title big across the middle, glitching in; the end title cracks into place with a flash. Performance: the roof compiles the ash shader during its load; the loading and death screens stop drawing the moment they are gone. |
| 16 | A Ghost of Tsushima-style selector, bottom right, for the sphere in use | **The sphere wheel** (`src/ui/sphere-wheel.js`, `preview/sphere-wheel.html` to look at it alone): an X of fine lines with the three spheres on its points as medallions - glass a crystal, cryo a snowflake, shock a bolt; the one loaded enlarged in its colour on a lit diamond panel that slides to it as you switch (Q / E or the wheel), the spheres you have (red when you can't afford a shot), and its name and cost. In every level; the Skyline's speed gauges and the toasts moved up out of its way. |
| 17 | Remove the Skyline's minimap; give the roof one | The Skyline's minimap (and its `M` key and View option) is gone. The roof has a **tactical map**, top right (`meltdown/roof-map.js`): the whole roof from straight above through its own orthographic camera, drawn picture-in-picture from flat icons on a layer the game's camera never sees - the parapets and the open ledges (red), cover, hatches, the helipad and the lift hut, you (an arrow pointing where you aim), patients (red, a brute bigger), scientists (amber), the supply drop (pulsing green), and the helicopter and its ladder (a pulsing cyan ring while it waits). |
